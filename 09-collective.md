# The Collective

Evidism is a collective. It belongs to its members together, and no person, office, or group owns it, speaks for it on their own authority, or decides for it alone.

## Roles facilitate

Every role in Evidism exists to facilitate. Those who hold a role help unify the members, carry the purpose forward, and support the people they serve.

A role carries responsibility, not power. No one who holds a role may dictate to members or make a decision on their behalf. Roles are held for a time, open to anyone, and answerable to the members.

## Decisions are shared

Decisions that affect members are made with them, in the open. Anyone affected may take part.

Agreement will rarely be unanimous. That is human, and it is not a failure. In every matter, the aim is common ground: a decision that as many members as possible can accept and live with, even when it is not each member's first choice.

Common ground is reached when at least 75% of the members taking part agree in a vote.

- **Every view is heard** before a vote is taken.
- **Disagreement is stated plainly** and recorded alongside the decision.
- **Without common ground, nothing changes.** Members keep reasoning together and may vote again.
- **Decisions stay open to revision** when evidence or circumstances change.

When a decision cannot wait, those affected may make a temporary one. It is recorded as temporary and revisited when time allows.

## When harm is done

Anyone who causes harm or acts dishonestly in a role steps down from it when members reach common ground that they should. A station may ask anyone who causes harm to stay away from its gatherings, and always protects children and vulnerable people first.

## Resources

Anything given to Evidism is used openly for its members and its purpose. No one profits from it.

## No one decides alone

No member, role, or group may decide alone for Evidism. A decision made by one on behalf of all is not a decision of Evidism.
