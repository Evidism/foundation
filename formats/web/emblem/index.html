The Emblem

The emblem of Evidism is a lowercase e inside an open ring, with a single point set into the opening.

  • The e is the first letter of evidence, the ground on which every belief rests.
  • The ring is the dial of an instrument, drawn as one line around what we measure.
  • The point at the top is a single measurement, set at the zero mark where every reading begins.

A child should be able to draw it from memory.
