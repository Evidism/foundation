Acceptance, Freedom, and Individual Rights

  • No harm
  • Where we stand

Evidism is founded on freedom of thought and on every person’s right to be fully human, free from the interference of others.

  • Acceptance. Every person is accepted as they are. No one is asked to become someone else in order to belong.
  • Freedom of thought. Each person is free to think, question, believe, and change their mind. No idea is forbidden from examination, and no person is punished for holding one.
  • Individuality. Individuality is critical. Each life is a single, unrepeatable instance, and its differences are to be protected rather than corrected.
  • The best possible life. We work toward a world where every person, member or not, can lead the best possible life.

No harm

Evidism does not support actions that knowingly harm others.

One person’s freedom does not extend to taking the same freedom from another.

Where we stand

We support these rights for our members, in our communities, across our regions, and globally. We support those who live by this purpose and those who serve it, whether or not they are Evidists.
