Evidism

Evidism (noun): from the Latin evidens, plain to see, from ex, out of, and videre, to see. The root of evidence.

Meaning is found in what is real, and measurement is how we come to know it.

Founding Document Version 1.0.0 21 November 2026

Contents

  1. 01 Preamble Evidism holds that meaning is found in what is real. The natural world, the things around us, and our measurable relationship to both are sufficient ground for existence, happiness, and purpose.
  2. 02 The Creed Reality can be known, in strides both large and small, and measurement is how we come to know it.
  3. 03 Non-Theism Evidism is non-theistic. It names no God, and the universe is our guidance.
  4. 04 The Tenets Seven tenets turn the creed into daily practice.
  5. 05 Ethics Evidism chooses three moral commitments: wellbeing, honesty, and stewardship.
  6. 06 Acceptance, Freedom, and Individual Rights Evidism is founded on freedom of thought and on every person’s right to be fully human, free from the interference of others.
  7. 07 The Calendar The Evidist year is marked by six astronomical observances and three days of measure.
  8. 08 Rituals and Practices Evidist rituals mark life’s passages with facts that are both true and moving.
  9. 09 The Collective Evidism is a collective. It belongs to its members together, and no person, office, or group owns it, speaks for it on their own authority, or decides for it alone.
  10. 10 Structure Evidism has no prophets and no clergy. Authority rests in evidence and in the review of peers. Every role below exists to facilitate, as set out in The Collective.
  11. 11 The Living Canon This document is expected to change. Its current version is shown on its title page.
  12. 12 The Emblem The emblem of Evidism is a lowercase e inside an open ring, with a single point set into the opening.
