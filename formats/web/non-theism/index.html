Evidism is non-theistic. It names no God, and the universe is our guidance.

On God

Evidism neither affirms nor denies a deity. It names none, worships none, and asks no member to do either.

No member is required or encouraged to give up beliefs held outside Evidism. What a member believes beyond this document is their own.

Evidism offers a method, not a test. Members apply it as far as they choose, and no one examines another’s private beliefs.

On origins and destiny

We do not claim to know where we come from or where we are going beyond what scientific evidence shows. Whatever lies past that evidence is a Lacuna, as described in the tenet of Uncertainty.

On spirit

Evidism looks for wonder in the universe itself. Attention to what is real is its spiritual practice.

On the meaning of life

Evidism does not hand down a single meaning of life. Guidance in life comes from reason and from each member’s own beliefs and ideals.
