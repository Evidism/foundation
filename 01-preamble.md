# Preamble

Evidism holds that meaning is found in what is real. The natural world, the things around us, and our measurable relationship to both are sufficient ground for existence, happiness, and purpose.

Many traditions rest their meaning on mythology and conjecture. Evidism rests it on what can be observed, measured, and shared. We do not claim certainty. We claim a method: look closely, measure honestly, and change our minds when the evidence does.

The purpose of Evidism is to find meaning, wonder, and community in what is real, and to leave the world better for understanding it.

This document founds Evidism, states what its members hold in common, and describes how it may be revised.
